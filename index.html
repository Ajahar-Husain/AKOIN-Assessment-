About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute specializing in digital, cyber, and technology risk roles within regulated, high-consequence environments. We bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application in the rapidly evolving landscape of digital risk management.

Through our strategic partnership with leading UK universities, we combine cutting-edge academic insight with real-world project experience. Our practitioners work on live industry challenges, ensuring that training translates directly into deployable skills for financial services, critical infrastructure, and other high-stakes sectors.

Our Service Model

Academy

Intensive training programs and bootcamps for students and professionals, covering digital risk, AI governance, and cybersecurity frameworks.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing intellectual property, future risk models, and AI governance frameworks to stay ahead of emerging digital threats.

Advisory Services

Helping organizations implement industry frameworks including NIST, ISO 27001, and NIS2 compliance standards.

Who We Serve

Our community comprises students, early-career professionals, and experienced practitioners seeking to advance their expertise in digital and cyber risk management. We specialize in serving sectors where risk management is mission-critical, including financial services, critical infrastructure, healthcare, and government agencies.

Whether you're a graduate looking to break into the field, a professional seeking upskilling opportunities, or an organization needing specialized talent, IDR provides the bridge between education and industry-ready expertise.
